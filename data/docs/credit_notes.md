# Credit Notes and Refunds

## When a credit note is allowed
A credit note may be issued for damaged goods, short deliveries or pricing errors. The credit note amount can never exceed the open or paid amount of the original invoice.

## Approval rules
Every credit note needs approval before it is booked in the ERP. Credit notes up to EUR 500 can be approved by the account manager. Credit notes above EUR 500 need approval from the head of finance.

## Processing time
Approved credit notes are booked within 2 business days and appear on the customer's next statement.
