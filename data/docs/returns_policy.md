# Returns Policy

## Return window
Customers can return unused products within 30 days of delivery. Custom-made and cut-to-length products cannot be returned.

## Damaged deliveries
Damage must be reported within 5 business days of delivery, with photos and the delivery note number. Late damage reports are reviewed case by case.

## Return shipping
For damaged or wrong deliveries we pay return shipping. For all other returns the customer pays return shipping and a 10% restocking fee.
