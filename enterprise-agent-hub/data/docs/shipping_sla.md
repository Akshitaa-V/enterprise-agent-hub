# Shipping and Service Levels

## Delivery times
Orders confirmed before 14:00 on a business day ship the same day. Standard delivery within Germany takes 1 to 2 business days, and delivery within the EU takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Express service
Express delivery within Germany arrives the next business day before 12:00 and costs EUR 25 per order.

## Support service levels
Priority 1 support tickets (production stopped) get a first response within 2 hours. Priority 2 tickets get a first response within 1 business day.
