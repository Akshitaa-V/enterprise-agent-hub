# Customer Onboarding

## New business customers
A new business customer needs a valid VAT ID and a signed framework contract before the first order on account. Until then, orders are prepaid.

## Credit limit
New customers start with a credit limit of EUR 10,000. The limit can be raised after 6 months without overdue invoices.

## Account manager
Each business customer is assigned one account manager, who is the first contact for orders, invoices and credit notes.
