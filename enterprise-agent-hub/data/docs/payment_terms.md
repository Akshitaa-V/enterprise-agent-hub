# Payment Terms

## Standard terms
Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Key accounts with a framework contract may be granted 45 days by the finance team.

## Late payment
An invoice becomes overdue on the first day after its due date. After 14 days overdue, the account manager sends a first reminder. After 30 days overdue, finance sends a second reminder and may put new orders for that customer on hold.

## Early payment discount
Customers who pay within 10 days of the invoice date receive a 2% early payment discount, provided the invoice is not already subject to a credit note.
